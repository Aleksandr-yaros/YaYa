# Minu Payment Solutions — response to letter dated September 4, 2026

**From:** BATGEREL Gerelkhuu, Chairwoman  
**To:** Mr. Iaroslavtsev Alexandr  
**Context:** Proposed acquisition of Telemedia Group LLC and Yaros Group LLC

## Original letter text

Dear Mr. Iaroslavtsev Alexandr

We are sending this response to your email dated September 4, 2026.

Thank you very much for your letter and for your continued cooperation and open dialogue throughout this process. We greatly value the constructive spirit in which both sides have approached these discussions, and we remain genuinely committed to reaching an agreement that works well for everyone involved.

Having carefully considered the points raised in your recent correspondence, we would be pleased to proceed with the due diligence of Yaros Group LLC once we have received your written confirmation of the following key principles. We have set these out in detail below so that our respective positions are as clear as possible going into the next stage of this process.

1. **Telemedia Group LLC:** Based on the on-site due diligence we have already conducted, we have determined the company's net profit to be KGS 33 million. We are prepared to accept the KGS 14 million of documented, non-recurring expenses that your side has confirmed will not recur, bringing the normalized net profit to KGS 47 million. On this basis, we are pleased to submit our final offer to acquire the company at this normalized amount multiplied by the previously agreed 2.8x multiple.

2. **Yaros Group LLC:** We will still need to carry out further on-site due diligence on Yaros Group LLC before finalizing our position, and we want to be transparent that we anticipate this process proceeding smoothly. Based on our current estimates, we expect Yaros Group LLC's normalized net profit to be approximately KGS 72 million, and we have no objection in principle to acquiring Yaros Group LLC at this normalized net profit (subject to mutual verification through due diligence) multiplied by the agreed 3.2x multiple. In the interest of full transparency, we also want to flag that we do not rule out the possibility that Telemedia Group LLC and Yaros Group's net profit figure may be adjusted again in the course of the due diligence on Yaros Group LLC, should the review reveal any related findings.

3. **Payment period:** We have given careful thought to the proposed payment timeline, and a two-month period is simply too short for us to responsibly accept, particularly given the scale of this transaction and our internal planning requirements. We would like to propose instead that the purchase price be paid in installments over a minimum period of six months. We believe this is a reasonable and mutually protective timeline. To further safeguard the interests of both parties and manage risk fairly, we propose using a bank escrow account for the transaction, with the associated costs shared equally on a 50/50 basis.

4. **Transfer of ownership:** We consider it essential that the transfer of the company begin immediately upon signing of the agreement. This is a standard and necessary element of a transaction of this nature, and we do not see a workable alternative to it. We hope you will appreciate that this is not a point of flexibility for us, but rather a basic requirement for moving forward together. Upon signing of the agreement, we will deposit USD 1 million into a joint escrow account, following which the company will be fully transferred to us. The remaining payments will then be made into the escrow account, and once the entire process has been completed, you will be able to withdraw the funds.

5. **Business continuity:** We want to reassure you and the team at both companies that, for a period of 12 months following completion, we will maintain the current employees' salaries and working conditions unchanged. We recognize how important stability is for the people who have built these businesses, and we are fully committed to a smooth and respectful transition for everyone involved. In this regard, we would like to reiterate that we will not accept or recognize any increases to employee salaries or benefits made prior to the transfer of the company that have not been agreed with us. Furthermore, we consider it essential that the companies' key employees remain with the business following completion, and we require your confirmation that such key employees will not leave the company to join or establish a competing business.

6. **Transaction costs:** We agree to share all costs related to the upcoming on-site due diligence and the transfer of the company equally on a 50/50 basis.

We want to be candid with you: this represents our final offer, and the terms set out above are not subject to further negotiation on our end. We have arrived at this position only after careful and good-faith consideration of everything discussed to date, and we believe it reflects a fair outcome for both sides.

Thank you again for your time, consideration, and continued partnership throughout this process. We look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,  
**BATGEREL Gerelkhuu**  
Chairwoman
